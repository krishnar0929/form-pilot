FloodSense Kogi

AI-powered flood prediction and situational awareness platform for Kogi State, Nigeria. Combines IoT river sensors, Sentinel-1 SAR satellite data, weather forecasts, and community reports via SMS/USSD. An LSTM model forecasts water levels 6–72 hours ahead; alerts go to emergency responders through a GIS dashboard and to residents via SMS in local languages. Offline-first design for low connectivity. Future: 3D digital twin.
